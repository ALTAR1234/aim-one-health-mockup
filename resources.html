Whitepaper · The method

The Recursive Human–Machine Outcome Loop™

Most AI tools make a recommendation and stop. AiM runs a continuous loop: the machine proposes, clinicians validate, action follows, outcomes are measured, and the system learns from every result, until the goal is reached.

What teaches the machine

Clinical guidelinesResearch & literatureClinical data & outcomesRadiology & pathologyPolicies & standardsExpert knowledgeBest practices
1

Human ↔ machine

The machine surfaces intelligence from the literature and the patient's own chart.

  • Recommendations
  • Risk scores
  • Questions & insights
2

Human ↔ human

Clinicians, specialists, payers and patients challenge, debate and agree.

  • Validate or reject
  • Modify & refine
  • Reach consensus
3

Action

Validated decisions become real action, at the bedside and beyond.

  • Treatment selected
  • Workflow updated
  • Resources allocated
4

Measure the outcome

Did it work? Every cycle is measured against what matters most.

  • Better outcomes?
  • Fewer complications?
  • Better experience?
5

Feed back

Outcomes become new teaching data: what worked, and what didn't.

  • Patterns & exceptions
  • Disagreements
  • New knowledge

The goal is a validated outcome.
