01 · Executive summary

The panel agrees on a preferred first-line therapy

In short

A third-generation EGFR TKI is the preferred first-line therapy. The PD-L1 result does not change that, because of the driver mutation.

For a treatment-naïve EGFR exon 19-deletion metastatic NSCLC, the council converges on a third-generation EGFR TKI as preferred first-line therapy, with the PD-L1 result not redirecting to front-line immunotherapy given the driver mutation. Two dissenting considerations, CNS surveillance cadence and the role of upfront local therapy for oligometastatic disease, are carried forward with explicit evidence.

03 · Evidence synthesis

What the evidence says

Pivotal RCT

In the referenced phase III trial, a third-generation EGFR TKI improved progression-free survival versus a first-generation TKI in EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC, with favorable CNS activity. Synthetic citation for demonstration.

Guideline

National guidelines recommend EGFR-directed therapy over immunotherapy alone when a sensitizing EGFR alteration is present, whatever the PD-L1 level. Synthetic citation for demonstration.

06 · Red-team challenge

We tried to break the recommendation

Challenge

Does PD-L1 TPS 40% justify adding immunotherapy front-line?

Resolution

The consensus holds. In EGFR-driven disease, front-line immunotherapy isn't supported and may raise toxicity risk near TKIs. Recommendation unchanged; reasoning documented.
