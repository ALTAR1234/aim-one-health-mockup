Questions

Good to know

Does AI make the decision about my care?

No. AI helps your care team see the evidence and the options. Your treating physician always makes the final clinical decision.

Which conditions do you cover?

Oncology is our first specialty. Additional specialties are coming soon.

What does it cost?

The Care Pathway Research Document has a standard value of $300. During early access it's free for the first 50 eligible patients.

Can I see an example first?

Yes. Our sample report uses a synthetic case, so you can see exactly what you'd receive.
